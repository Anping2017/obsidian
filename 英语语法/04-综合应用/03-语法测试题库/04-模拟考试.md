## 📚 考试内容

### 1. 基础语法测试 (25分)

#### 1.1 词性识别测试 (8分)
**题目1: 词性识别**
请识别下列单词的词性：
```
1. beautiful → 
2. quickly → 
3. and → 
4. the → 
5. five → 
6. in → 
7. who → 
8. my → 
```

**题目2: 词性转换**
请将下列单词转换为指定词性：
```
1. beauty (名词) → beautiful (形容词)
2. quick (形容词) → quickly (副词)
3. friend (名词) → friendly (形容词)
4. care (名词) → careful (形容词)
5. help (动词) → helpful (形容词)
6. use (动词) → useful (形容词)
7. danger (名词) → dangerous (形容词)
8. success (名词) → successful (形容词)
```

#### 1.2 句子成分测试 (8分)
**题目1: 句子成分识别**
请识别下列句子中的句子成分：
```
1. Tom is a student.
   主语: Tom, 谓语: is, 表语: a student

2. She reads books every day.
   主语: She, 谓语: reads, 宾语: books, 状语: every day

3. The book on the table is mine.
   主语: The book, 谓语: is, 表语: mine, 定语: on the table

4. I gave him a book yesterday.
   主语: I, 谓语: gave, 间接宾语: him, 直接宾语: a book, 状语: yesterday

5. He is reading a book in the library.
   主语: He, 谓语: is reading, 宾语: a book, 状语: in the library

6. The man who is talking is Tom.
   主语: The man, 谓语: is, 表语: Tom, 定语: who is talking

7. I know that he is a student.
   主语: I, 谓语: know, 宾语: that he is a student

8. If it rains, we will stay at home.
   条件状语: If it rains, 主语: we, 谓语: will stay, 状语: at home
```

#### 1.3 基本语法规则测试 (9分)
**题目1: 语法规则应用**
请应用基本语法规则完成下列句子：
```
1. 名词单复数变化:
   book → books
   child → children
   mouse → mice
   tooth → teeth
   foot → feet

2. 代词使用:
   I → my (物主代词)
   you → your (物主代词)
   he → his (物主代词)
   she → her (物主代词)
   it → its (物主代词)

3. 冠词使用:
   ___ book (a/an) → a book
   ___ apple (a/an) → an apple
   ___ sun (the) → the sun
   ___ moon (the) → the moon
```

### 2. 时态语态测试 (25分)

#### 2.1 时态测试 (15分)
**题目1: 时态选择**
请选择正确的时态：
```
1. Tom ___ (go) to school every day.
   答案: goes (一般现在时)

2. She ___ (read) a book now.
   答案: is reading (现在进行时)

3. I ___ (finish) my homework already.
   答案: have finished (现在完成时)

4. He ___ (study) English for 3 years.
   答案: has been studying (现在完成进行时)

5. We ___ (visit) Beijing last year.
   答案: visited (一般过去时)

6. They ___ (play) football at 3 PM yesterday.
   答案: were playing (过去进行时)

7. She ___ (finish) her work before I came.
   答案: had finished (过去完成时)

8. Tom ___ (study) English for 2 years before he came here.
   答案: had been studying (过去完成进行时)

9. We ___ (go) to Beijing tomorrow.
   答案: will go (一般将来时)

10. He ___ (work) at 3 PM tomorrow.
   答案: will be working (将来进行时)

11. I ___ (finish) my work by 5 PM tomorrow.
   答案: will have finished (将来完成时)

12. She ___ (study) English for 5 years by next year.
   答案: will have been studying (将来完成进行时)

13. If it ___ (rain), we will stay at home.
   答案: rains (一般现在时)

14. If I ___ (be) you, I would help him.
   答案: were (虚拟语气)

15. I wish I ___ (be) a bird.
   答案: were (虚拟语气)
```

#### 2.2 语态测试 (10分)
**题目1: 语态转换**
请将下列句子转换为被动语态：
```
1. 原句: Tom broke the window.
   转换: The window was broken by Tom.

2. 原句: She is reading a book.
   转换: A book is being read by her.

3. 原句: I have finished my homework.
   转换: My homework has been finished by me.

4. 原句: We will visit Beijing tomorrow.
   转换: Beijing will be visited by us tomorrow.

5. 原句: They were playing football.
   转换: Football was being played by them.

6. 原句: He had finished his work.
   转换: His work had been finished by him.

7. 原句: She will be reading a book.
   转换: A book will be being read by her.

8. 原句: I will have finished my work.
   转换: My work will have been finished by me.

9. 原句: They have been studying English.
   转换: English has been being studied by them.

10. 原句: We will have been working for 5 years.
   转换: Work will have been being done by us for 5 years.
```

### 3. 从句测试 (25分)

#### 3.1 名词性从句测试 (8分)
**题目1: 名词性从句识别**
请识别下列句子中的名词性从句类型：
```
1. That he is a student is true.
   类型: 主语从句

2. I know that he is a student.
   类型: 宾语从句

3. The fact is that he is a student.
   类型: 表语从句

4. The fact that he is a student is true.
   类型: 同位语从句

5. What he said is true.
   类型: 主语从句

6. I don't know what he said.
   类型: 宾语从句

7. The question is what he said.
   类型: 表语从句

8. The question what he said is difficult.
   类型: 同位语从句
```

#### 3.2 定语从句测试 (8分)
**题目1: 定语从句识别**
请识别下列句子中的定语从句类型：
```
1. The book that I bought is good.
   类型: 限制性定语从句

2. The book, which I bought, is good.
   类型: 非限制性定语从句

3. The man who is talking is Tom.
   类型: 限制性定语从句

4. The man, who is talking, is Tom.
   类型: 非限制性定语从句

5. The place where he lives is here.
   类型: 限制性定语从句

6. The place, where he lives, is here.
   类型: 非限制性定语从句

7. The time when he came is 3 PM.
   类型: 限制性定语从句

8. The time, when he came, is 3 PM.
   类型: 非限制性定语从句
```

#### 3.3 状语从句测试 (9分)
**题目1: 状语从句识别**
请识别下列句子中的状语从句类型：
```
1. When he came, I was reading.
   类型: 时间状语从句

2. Where he lives, there are many trees.
   类型: 地点状语从句

3. Because it is raining, we stay at home.
   类型: 原因状语从句

4. If it rains, we will stay at home.
   类型: 条件状语从句

5. So that he can pass the exam, he studies hard.
   类型: 目的状语从句

6. He is so tired that he can't work.
   类型: 结果状语从句

7. Although it is raining, we go out.
   类型: 让步状语从句

8. He is taller than I am.
   类型: 比较状语从句

9. He did as I told him.
   类型: 方式状语从句
```

### 4. 高级语法测试 (25分)

#### 4.1 非谓语动词测试 (10分)
**题目1: 非谓语动词选择**
请选择正确的非谓语动词形式：
```
1. I want ___ (go) to school.
   答案: to go (不定式)

2. I enjoy ___ (read) books.
   答案: reading (动名词)

3. The book ___ (write) by Tom is good.
   答案: written (过去分词)

4. The man ___ (talk) is Tom.
   答案: talking (现在分词)

5. ___ (finish) his homework, he went to bed.
   答案: Having finished (现在分词)

6. The work ___ (finish), we went home.
   答案: finished (过去分词)

7. I saw him ___ (read) a book.
   答案: reading (现在分词)

8. I want the work ___ (finish) by 5 PM.
   答案: to be finished (不定式被动)

9. The book is worth ___ (read).
   答案: reading (动名词)

10. He is too young ___ (go) to school.
   答案: to go (不定式)
```

#### 4.2 特殊句式测试 (8分)
**题目1: 特殊句式识别**
请识别下列句子中的特殊句式：
```
1. Never have I seen such a thing.
   类型: 倒装句

2. It was Tom that broke the window.
   类型: 强调句

3. Tom likes apples, and Tom likes oranges.
   类型: 省略句

4. Weather permitting, we will go out.
   类型: 独立主格

5. The work finished, we went home.
   类型: 独立主格

6. The meeting over, we left.
   类型: 独立主格

7. The sun shining, we went out.
   类型: 独立主格

8. The book that Tom bought is good.
   类型: 省略句
```

#### 4.3 语用规则测试 (7分)
**题目1: 语用规则应用**
请应用语用规则完成下列句子：
```
1. 主谓一致:
   The team ___ playing well. (is/are)
   答案: is

2. 反义疑问句:
   Tom is a student, ___?
   答案: isn't he

3. 直接间接引语:
   "I am a student," Tom said.
   转换: Tom said that he was a student.

4. 语域语用:
   正式场合: I would like to request...
   非正式场合: I want to ask...

5. 礼貌用语:
   请求: Could you please help me?
   拒绝: I'm sorry, but I can't.

6. 感谢表达:
   正式: I would like to express my gratitude.
   非正式: Thanks a lot.

7. 道歉表达:
   正式: I sincerely apologize for...
   非正式: Sorry about that.
```
