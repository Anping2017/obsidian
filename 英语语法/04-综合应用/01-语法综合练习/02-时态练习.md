### 1. 现在时态群练习

#### 1.1 一般现在时练习
**练习1: 动词形式变化**
请将下列动词改为一般现在时的正确形式：
```
1. I (go) to school every day. → I go to school every day.
2. She (like) apples. → She likes apples.
3. They (play) football. → They play football.
4. Tom (study) English. → Tom studies English.
5. We (work) hard. → We work hard.
6. The sun (rise) in the east. → The sun rises in the east.
7. Water (boil) at 100°C. → Water boils at 100°C.
8. Birds (fly) in the sky. → Birds fly in the sky.
9. The earth (revolve) around the sun. → The earth revolves around the sun.
10. Time (pass) quickly. → Time passes quickly.
```

请选择正确的时间状语：
```

3. They play football ___. (at the moment/on Sundays/last Sunday)
4. Tom studies English ___. (currently/every day/next week)
5. We work hard ___. (always/just now/next month)
```

**答案**：
1. I go to school every day.
2. She reads books every morning.
3. They play football on Sundays.
4. Tom studies English every day.
5. We work hard always.

### 4. 时态综合练习

#### 4.1 时态选择练习
**练习1: 时态选择**
请根据语境选择正确的时态：
```
1. I ___ to school every day. (go/goes/went/will go)
   答案: go (一般现在时，表示习惯)

2. She ___ a book now. (read/reads/is reading/will read)
   答案: is reading (现在进行时，表示正在进行的动作)

3. They ___ the museum last week. (visit/visits/visited/will visit)
   答案: visited (一般过去时，表示过去的动作)

4. Tom ___ English for five years. (study/studies/has studied/will study)
   答案: has studied (现在完成时，表示从过去开始持续到现在的动作)

5. We ___ hard when the boss came. (work/works/were working/will work)
   答案: were working (过去进行时，表示过去某个时间正在进行的动作)
```

#### 4.2 时态转换练习
**练习1: 时态转换**
请将下列句子转换为指定时态：
```
1. 原句: Tom reads books. (一般现在时)
   转换为一般过去时: Tom read books.
   转换为一般将来时: Tom will read books.
   转换为现在进行时: Tom is reading books.
   转换为现在完成时: Tom has read books.

2. 原句: She is writing a letter. (现在进行时)
   转换为一般过去时: She was writing a letter.
   转换为一般将来时: She will be writing a letter.
   转换为一般现在时: She writes letters.
   转换为现在完成时: She has written a letter.

3. 原句: They played football. (一般过去时)
   转换为一般现在时: They play football.
   转换为一般将来时: They will play football.
   转换为过去进行时: They were playing football.
   转换为过去完成时: They had played football.
```

#### 4.3 时态错误纠正练习
**练习1: 时态错误纠正**
请纠正下列句子中的时态错误：
```
1. ❌ I am go to school every day.
   ✅ I go to school every day.

2. ❌ She reads a book now.
   ✅ She is reading a book now.

3. ❌ They play football yesterday.
```

## 📊 练习总结

### 5. 练习要点
1. **时态识别**: 能够准确识别不同时态
2. **时态应用**: 能够正确应用不同时态
3. **时态转换**: 能够进行时态转换
4. **错误纠正**: 能够发现和纠正时态错误

### 6. 练习方法
1. **循序渐进**: 从简单到复杂
2. **反复练习**: 通过反复练习巩固
3. **对比分析**: 通过对比分析理解
4. **实际应用**: 在实际语境中应用
