```

6. be → to be
7. take → to take
```


### 2. 特殊句式练习

#### 2.1 倒装句练习
**练习1: 完全倒装**
请将下列句子改为完全倒装：
```
1. 原句: The book is on the table.
   转换: On the table is the book.

2. 原句: The students are in the classroom.
   转换: In the classroom are the students.

3. 原句: The car is in the garage.
   转换: In the garage is the car.

4. 原句: The flowers are in the garden.
   转换: In the garden are the flowers.

5. 原句: The birds are in the tree.
   转换: In the tree are the birds.
```

**练习2: 部分倒装**

```

2. 原句: He can hardly speak English.
   转换: Hardly can he speak English.
```

### 3. 语用规则练习

#### 3.1 主谓一致练习
**练习1: 主谓一致识别**
请选择正确的主谓一致：
```
1. The team ___ playing well. (is/are)
   答案: is (强调整体)

2. The team ___ arguing about the strategy. (is/are)
   答案: are (强调个体)

3. Everyone ___ here. (is/are)
   答案: is (不定代词用单数)

4. All ___ welcome. (is/are)
   答案: are (all用复数)

5. Tom and Jerry ___ friends. (is/are)
   答案: are (and连接用复数)
```

**练习2: 主谓一致错误纠正**
请纠正下列句子中的主谓一致错误：
```
1. ❌ The team are playing well.
   ✅ The team is playing well.

2. ❌ Everyone are here.
   ✅ Everyone is here.

3. ❌ All is welcome.
   ✅ All are welcome.

4. ❌ Tom and Jerry is friends.
   ✅ Tom and Jerry are friends.

5. ❌ The book and the pen is on the table.
   ✅ The book and the pen are on the table.
```
