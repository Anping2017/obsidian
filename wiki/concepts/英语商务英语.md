# 英语商务英语

## 定义

商务英语(Business English)是国际商业活动场景中使用的英语变体,涵盖商务邮件、电话会议、商务谈判、销售演示、产品文档、商业报告等场景。与学术英语的"严谨客观"、日常英语的"自由灵活"不同,商务英语强调**简洁专业、礼貌得体、目标导向**——既要传递清晰信息,又要维护商业关系,还要推动具体行动。商务英语是国际化职场的基本通行证。

## 核心要点

### 商务邮件结构

**主题(Subject Line)**:简明扼要,15 词以内,体现核心目的:
- 好:*Quarterly Report Q3 - Action Required by Oct 15*
- 差:*Hello* / *About the meeting*

**称呼(Greeting)**:
- 正式(初次/非熟):*Dear Mr. Smith / Dear Ms. Lee*
- 半正式(有交往):*Hi John*(无姓)/ *Dear John*
- 团队邮件:*Hi Team / Dear All*
- 不知姓名:*To Whom It May Concern* / *Dear Sir/Madam*

**开场(Opening)**:
- *I hope this email finds you well.*(经典开场)
- *Thank you for your email regarding...*(回复)
- *I am writing to inquire about...*(主动询问)
- *Following up on our conversation last Tuesday, ...*(跟进)

**正文(Body)**:简洁分段,每段一个核心点。使用 bullet points 列举多项内容。

**行动呼吁(Call to Action)**:
- *Could you please confirm by Friday?*
- *Please let me know if this works for you.*
- *Looking forward to your reply.*

**结尾(Closing)**:
- 正式:*Sincerely, Yours faithfully, Best regards*
- 半正式:*Best, Cheers, Thanks*

**签名(Signature)**:姓名 + 职位 + 公司 + 联系方式

### 商务沟通的礼貌策略

**间接请求** > 直接命令:
- 差:*Send me the file.*(命令式)
- 好:*Could you please send me the file?* / *Would you mind sending me the file?*

**软化否定**:
- 差:*That's wrong.*
- 好:*I'm not sure that's quite right.* / *Could there be a misunderstanding?*

**承认不便**:
- *I understand this may not be ideal, but...*
- *I apologize for the late reply...*

**致谢前置**:
- *Thank you for your patience...*
- *I appreciate your help with this matter.*

### 高频商务表达

**会议**:
- *Let's get started.* / *Let's go around the room.*
- *Could we schedule a follow-up meeting?*
- *We need to align on this issue.*
- *To recap, we agreed that...*
- *Action items / Next steps...*

**演示/汇报**:
- *Today I'd like to walk you through...*
- *Let me give you a brief overview.*
- *As you can see from this slide, ...*
- *To summarize, the key takeaways are...*

**谈判**:
- *We're looking at $X for this proposal.*
- *Is there any flexibility on the price?*
- *We'd like to find a mutually beneficial solution.*
- *Let me consult with my team and get back to you.*

**电子邮件常用表达**:
- *Per our discussion, ...*(正如我们讨论的)
- *FYI(For Your Information)*
- *EOD(End of Day)*
- *ASAP(As Soon As Possible)*
- *Loop me in.*(把我加入信息流)
- *Touch base.*(简单沟通)
- *Circle back.*(再回过头讨论)
- *Move the needle.*(产生显著影响)

### 商务文档类型

**提案(Proposal)**:Executive Summary - Background - Solution - Cost - Timeline - Team
**报告(Report)**:Title - Summary - Findings - Analysis - Recommendations - Appendix
**会议纪要(Meeting Minutes)**:Date/Attendees/Agenda - Discussion - Decisions - Action Items
**商业计划书(Business Plan)**:Vision - Market Analysis - Strategy - Financials - Team
**新闻稿(Press Release)**:Headline - Lead Paragraph - Body - Boilerplate - Contact

### 跨文化商务沟通

**美式 vs 英式商务**:美式更直接、个人主义、行动导向;英式更含蓄、间接、关注体面。
**高语境 vs 低语境文化**:东亚高语境(意会),欧美低语境(明示)。中国商务人士与英美沟通需更明示。
**时间观**:严格守时是基本职业素养。
**关系导向 vs 任务导向**:某些文化(中、日、阿拉伯)重关系建立,英美文化偏直接进入业务。

### 电话与视频会议

**接听/开始**:
- *Hello, this is [Name] from [Company]. How can I help you?*
- *Hi everyone, can you all hear me?*

**听不清/请求重复**:
- *Sorry, could you repeat that?*
- *I'm sorry, you're breaking up.*
- *Could you speak a bit more slowly please?*

**留言**:
- *I'm calling regarding... / about...*
- *Could you please call me back at...?*

**结束**:
- *Thank you for your time.*
- *Let's stay in touch.*
- *I look forward to working together.*

### 中国职场人士的商务英语挑战

- **过度礼貌或过度直接**:语境拿捏失衡
- **逐字翻译中文表达**:"客气"翻成 polite 可能丢失含义
- **缩略词不熟**:FYI/EOD/ETA/KPI/ROI 等
- **文化错误**:称呼 first name vs last name、节假日祝福、礼物
- **表达模糊**:"我们应该会..."→ 应改为 "We will..." 或 "We may..." 明示确定性

应对:多看英文商务剧(*The Office*, *Suits*, *Mad Men*)、订阅 HBR(Harvard Business Review)、参加 Toastmasters、找跨文化职场导师。

## 和其他概念的关系

- 商务英语建立在 [[英语写作结构]] 通用规则上,但更简洁高效。
- 邮件体现 [[英语口语表达]] 中礼貌策略 — 用情态动词的过去式表更高礼貌(详见 [[英语情态动词]])。
- 与 [[英语学术英语]] 形成对比 — 商务偏行动,学术偏论证;商务用词更简洁,学术用词更正式。
- 商务高频短语动词如 *touch base*, *circle back*, *follow up* 涉及 [[英语习语与搭配]]。
- 跨文化部分与 [[英语英美文化]] 紧密关联。
- 在 [[英语 CEFR 等级]] 中,流利商务英语对应 B2-C1。
