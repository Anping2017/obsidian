# 从句练习

## 🎯 学习目标
- 巩固从句语法知识
- 提高从句应用能力
- 通过练习加深对从句的理解

## 📚 练习内容

### 1. 名词性从句练习

#### 1.1 主语从句练习
**练习1: 主语从句识别**
请识别下列句子中的主语从句：
```
1. What he said is true.
   主语从句: What he said
   主句: is true

2. That he is honest is obvious.
   主语从句: That he is honest
   主句: is obvious

3. Whether he will come is unknown.
   主语从句: Whether he will come
   主句: is unknown

4. Who will win the game is uncertain.
   主语从句: Who will win the game
   主句: is uncertain

5. Where he lives is a secret.
   主语从句: Where he lives
   主句: is a secret
```

**练习2: 主语从句转换**
请将下列句子转换为主语从句：
```
1. 原句: His words are true.
   转换: What he said is true.

2. 原句: His honesty is obvious.
   转换: That he is honest is obvious.

3. 原句: His coming is unknown.
   转换: Whether he will come is unknown.

4. 原句: The winner is uncertain.
   转换: Who will win the game is uncertain.

5. 原句: His address is a secret.
   转换: Where he lives is a secret.
```

**练习3: 主语从句填空**
请选择正确的主语从句连接词：
```
1. ___ he said is true. (What/That/Which)
   答案: What

2. ___ he is honest is obvious. (What/That/Which)
   答案: That

3. ___ he will come is unknown. (What/That/Whether)
   答案: Whether

4. ___ will win the game is uncertain. (What/Who/Which)
   答案: Who

5. ___ he lives is a secret. (What/Where/Which)
   答案: Where
```

### 2. 定语从句练习

```

   先行词: The book

2. The man who is talking is Tom.
   定语从句: who is talking
   先行词: The man

3. The place where he lives is here.
   定语从句: where he lives
   先行词: The place

4. The time when he came is 3 PM.
   定语从句: when he came
   先行词: The time

5. The reason why he left is unknown.
   定语从句: why he left
   先行词: The reason
```

**练习2: 限制性定语从句转换**
请将下列句子转换为限制性定语从句：
```

   转换: The book that I bought is good.

   转换: The man who is talking is Tom.

3. 原句: He lives in a place. The place is here.
   转换: The place where he lives is here.

4. 原句: He came at a time. The time is 3 PM.
   转换: The time when he came is 3 PM.

5. 原句: He left for a reason. The reason is unknown.
   转换: The reason why he left is unknown.
```

### 5. 练习要点
1. **从句识别**: 能够准确识别不同类型从句

### 6. 练习方法
1. **循序渐进**: 从简单到复杂
2. **反复练习**: 通过反复练习巩固
3. **对比分析**: 通过对比分析理解
4. **实际应用**: 在实际语境中应用
